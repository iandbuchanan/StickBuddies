STICKBUDDIES
============

Alan Becker style stick figures (TSC, Red, Green, Blue, Yellow) that live on your desktop.
They have their own homemade AI brain (a neural network written in JavaScript, no internet needed).
They explore, stand on your windows, play tag, watch your videos and games with you, dance to music,
and even write their own code. They start as beginners and learn by themselves.

WHAT YOU NEED
  - A Windows 10 or 11 computer
  - Node.js (free) - get the "LTS" version from https://nodejs.org
  - About 300 MB of free space and an internet connection for the first setup

HOW TO SET IT UP (only once)
  1. Unzip this folder somewhere (for example your Desktop).
  2. Install Node.js if you don't have it.
  3. Double-click  "SETUP (run this first).bat"  and wait until it says "All set!"

HOW TO USE IT
  - Start:  double-click "Start StickBuddies.bat"
  - Stop:   double-click "Stop StickBuddies.bat"  (or press Ctrl+Alt+Q)
  - Ctrl+Alt+B  = see their brains (what they're thinking and learning)
  - Ctrl+Alt+H  = hide / show them
  - Ctrl+Alt+M  = music on / off
  - Pick them up with your mouse (be gentle - if you fling them, they stop trusting you!)
  - Put your own music files in the "music" folder and they'll dance to it.
  - "Run Their Code.bat" runs the programs they write.

GOOD TO KNOW
  - They only LOOK at where your windows are and LISTEN to your computer's sound (to hear music).
    Nothing is recorded or sent anywhere.
  - They remember what they learn in memory.json. Delete it to make them beginners again.
  - The first time, Windows might ask "Do you want to allow this?" - that's normal, click Allow.
